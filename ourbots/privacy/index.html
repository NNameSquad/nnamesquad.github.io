Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all Discord bots, applications, and related services operated or managed by us.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all Discord bots, applications, and related services operated or managed by us (collectively referred to as the "Bots").

By using any of our Bots, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Message and Command Data

Our Bots primarily operate through Discord slash commands and other Discord interactions.

Our Bots generally do not read or store messages unless message content is required for a specific feature, command, or function.

Some Bots may use older text-based command systems. In such cases, a Bot may process a message when necessary to recognize or execute a command, when the Bot is mentioned or tagged, or when otherwise required for its functionality.

3. Data We May Process

Depending on the Bot and its features, we may process or log information such as:

  • Discord server information.
  • Discord user identifiers when required for a feature.
  • Information about interactions with our Bots.
  • Event participation.
  • Leaderboards and rankings.
  • Bug-hunter or similar event participation.
  • Server boost-related statistics.
  • Bot usage statistics.
  • Information necessary for troubleshooting, moderation, security, or debugging.
  • Other information required to provide specific Bot features.

Not every Bot collects or processes all of the information listed above.

4. Personal Data

We aim to minimize the amount of personal data processed by our Bots.

We do not intentionally collect or permanently store unnecessary personal information such as message history, profile pictures, usernames, or other personal information unless it is required for a specific feature or service.

Where information can be obtained directly from Discord when needed, we may access it in real time rather than permanently storing it.

Information may be temporarily processed or included in technical logs when necessary for the operation, security, troubleshooting, or maintenance of a Bot.

5. Purpose of Data Processing

Information processed by our Bots may be used for:

  • Providing Bot functionality.
  • Executing commands and interactions.
  • Operating events and competitions.
  • Maintaining leaderboards and statistics.
  • Counting server boosts or other server-related statistics.
  • Detecting and investigating bugs or technical issues.
  • Preventing abuse or misuse of our Bots.
  • Maintaining security and reliability.
  • Understanding Bot usage and improving our services.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Data is stored only when reasonably necessary for the operation of a Bot or one of its features.

Examples of information that may be retained include:

  • Event leaderboards.
  • Event statistics.
  • Boost counts.
  • Configuration information.
  • Other data required for a Bot to function correctly.

When stored information is no longer necessary, it may be deleted. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is used.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

We do not intentionally share collected data with unrelated third parties.

However, information may be processed by third-party services that are necessary to operate, host, develop, or manage our Bots.

8. Third-Party Services

Our Bots may use third-party services, platforms, APIs, hosting providers, or Bot development services to provide their functionality.

Examples may include services such as BotGhost, Bot Designer Discord, hosting providers, databases, analytics services, or other infrastructure providers.

These services may process or store information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Our use of a third-party service does not mean that we control how that service processes data. Users should review the relevant third party's privacy policy when appropriate.

9. Discord

Our Bots operate through Discord and are subject to Discord's platform and infrastructure.

Information made available through Discord may be processed by Discord independently of our Bots.

Our Bots only access information that is available to them through Discord and that is necessary for their functionality.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Access

Access to stored Bot data is limited to the Bot owner/developer and, where necessary, authorized staff or administrators responsible for operating or supporting the relevant Bot or community.

Access is granted only when reasonably necessary for maintenance, moderation, troubleshooting, security, or other operational purposes.

12. Server Membership

Some of our Bots may process limited information related to membership in Discord servers where the Bots operate.

If a user is not using one of our Bots and is not otherwise involved with a server, event, or feature operated by us, we generally do not intentionally retain information about that user.

Specific Bots may have different requirements depending on their functionality.

13. User Requests

If you have questions about information processed by one of our Bots, you may contact us through the relevant Bot's official support server or community.

When contacting us, please provide enough information for us to identify the relevant Bot and understand your request.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our Bots, services, data practices, or third-party services change.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy applies to all Bots covered by this policy.

Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.

15. Contact

For questions regarding our Bots or this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the official support server associated with the relevant Bot.

If a specific Bot has a dedicated support server or contact method, that information may be provided alongside the Bot's documentation or website.
